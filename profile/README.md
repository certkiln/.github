<p align="center"><b>Simple, self-hosted certificate management for x509 and SSH.</b></p>

---

Certkiln is a web app for issuing and managing certificates without a PKI
team. Start with the basics, then grow into enterprise features.

**Planned features**

- 🔥 **x509 certificates**: self-service issuing, renewal, and revocation from your own CA
- 🔑 **SSH certificates**: short-lived user and host certificates instead of scattered `authorized_keys`
- 🧱 **Simple by default**: sensible settings out of the box, with advanced options when you need them
- 🏢 **Enterprise-ready later**: SCEP and other enterprise integrations are on the roadmap

> **Status:** early development. Nothing is ready for production yet. Watch this
> organization to follow along.

**Reporting a security issue?** Please don't open a public issue. See our
[security policy](https://github.com/certkiln/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
